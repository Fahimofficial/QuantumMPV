What it brings

🎨 Deep customization

25+ themes, AMOLED variants, Material 3 UI and configurable player controls.

🖐️ Powerful gestures

Tap seeking, swipe seeking, dynamic speed, subtitle controls, zoom and volume boost.

📺 HDR & shaders

Advanced HDR pipelines, GPU debanding, smart render backend selection and shader support.

🔥 Thermal aware

Adaptive shader budgets and playback optimizations designed to reduce unnecessary device load.

📝 Subtitle tools

Flexible subtitle controls with online search, styling and experimental speech-to-subtitle support.

⚡ Hybrid playback

Native Android Media3 integration alongside the libmpv playback engine.
