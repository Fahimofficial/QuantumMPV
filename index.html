What it brings

The important parts, without turning the homepage into a manual.

Playback

⚡ Hybrid media engine

libmpv at the core, with native Android Media3 integration for a flexible playback stack.

Controls

🖐️ Powerful gestures

Seeking, speed control, subtitle gestures, zoom, volume boost and configurable player actions.

Video

📺 HDR & shaders

Advanced HDR pipelines, GPU debanding, smart render backends and shader support.

Performance

🔥 Thermal aware

Adaptive shader budgets and playback optimizations designed to reduce unnecessary device load.

Libraries

🎵 Video, music & servers

Local media plus integrations for Jellyfin, Navidrome/Subsonic, playlists and richer library workflows.

Network

🌐 More ways to watch

SMB, FTP, WebDAV, Cast, direct streams and other media sources are part of the wider project.

Subtitles

📝 Serious subtitle tools

Dual subtitles, styling, online search, font management and experimental speech-to-subtitle support.

Customization

🎨 Make it yours

25+ themes, AMOLED variants, configurable controls, orientations and player behavior.
